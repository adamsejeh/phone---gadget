Privacy Policy

We respect your personal data and are committed to protecting it. Here's how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

← Back to Store

📋 Information We Collect

When you browse, register, or make a purchase on M-Accessories & Gadgets, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you create an account or place an order.
  • Payment Data: Billing address and payment method details processed securely through our third-party payment providers. We do not store your card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage Data: Browsing behaviour, pages visited, products viewed, device type, browser information, and IP address for analytics and improving your experience.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use cookies and browser local storage to remember your cart, preferences, and login session.

🎯 How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us deliver the best possible shopping experience:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders, including shipping and warranty claims.
  • Sending order confirmations, delivery updates, and customer service responses.
  • Personalising product recommendations and promotional content based on your browsing history.
  • Improving our website design, product catalogue, and overall user experience.
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent transactions and unauthorised access.
  • Sending promotional newsletters and exclusive deals (only if you subscribe — you can unsubscribe anytime).

🛡️ Data Protection & Security

We take your data security very seriously. All sensitive information is transmitted using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption. Our systems are regularly audited and updated to protect against unauthorised access, data breaches, and cyber threats.

Payment processing is handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment providers, ensuring your financial data is never stored directly on our servers.

🤝 Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited data with:

  • Logistics & Delivery Partners: Name and shipping address to fulfil deliveries.
  • Payment Processors: Transaction details necessary to complete payments securely.
  • Analytics Providers: Anonymised usage data to help us improve the store experience.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

🍪 Cookies Policy

M-Accessories & Gadgets uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your shopping cart and login session across visits.
  • Analyse traffic patterns and user engagement to improve site performance.
  • Deliver relevant promotional content and ads.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the store.

✅ Your Rights

As a valued customer, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate information in your account.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@m-gadgets.com.

👶 Children's Privacy

Our store is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, or legal requirements. When significant changes are made, we will notify registered users via email and display a notice on the website. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
