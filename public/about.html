📖 Our Story

M-Accessories & Gadgets was founded with a simple but powerful mission: to bring premium-quality technology and gadgets to everyday people at fair, accessible prices. We saw a gap in the market — customers were paying top prices but getting substandard products with no warranty support. We set out to change that.

Today, we're one of the most trusted tech accessory brands, serving over 125,000 satisfied customers across Nigeria and West Africa. Every product in our catalogue is handpicked, rigorously tested, and backed by our industry-leading warranty — because we believe that when you invest in tech, it should work perfectly, last long, and look great while doing it.

💎 Why Our Products Are the Best Quality

Quality isn't just a buzzword for us — it's built into every step of our process:

  • Direct Manufacturer Partnerships: We source directly from authorised manufacturers and brand partners, eliminating middlemen and ensuring 100% genuine products.
  • Multi-Stage Quality Testing: Every batch undergoes rigorous testing — from stress testing power banks at full discharge cycles, to drop-testing cases, to audio calibration for earbuds — before reaching your hands.
  • Premium Materials Only: Carbon fibre, aircraft-grade aluminium, genuine leather, sapphire glass, Kevlar aramid — we never compromise on build quality.
  • International Certifications: Our products carry CE, FCC, RoHS, and MFi (Made for iPhone) certifications where applicable.
  • Real Customer Ratings: With an average of 4.9 out of 5 stars across all products, our quality speaks for itself. Every review on our store is from a verified buyer.

📋 What Our Warranty Covers

  • Manufacturing Defects: Any fault arising from the manufacturing process, including dead pixels, battery failures, circuit malfunctions, and material defects.
  • Battery Degradation: If your battery capacity drops below 80% within the warranty period, we replace it free of charge.
  • Component Failures: Charging ports, buttons, speakers, microphones, and internal components that fail under normal use.
  • Free Replacement: If a product cannot be repaired, we provide a full replacement or store credit — no questions asked.
  • Express Claim Processing: Warranty claims are processed within 48 hours of submission. We respect your time.

Not covered: Physical damage from drops/accidents, water damage (unless the product is rated IP68), unauthorised modifications, or cosmetic wear.

🔧 How to Claim Your Warranty

Filing a warranty claim with M-Gadgets is simple and fast:

  • Step 1: Contact us via email at warranty@m-gadgets.com or call +234 800 GADGETS.
  • Step 2: Provide your order number, product name, and a brief description or photo of the issue.
  • Step 3: Our team will review your claim within 24 hours and issue a resolution (repair, replacement, or credit).
  • Step 4: Ship the item to our service centre (we cover return shipping on Platinum-tier products).
  • Step 5: Receive your repaired/replaced product within 5-7 business days.
